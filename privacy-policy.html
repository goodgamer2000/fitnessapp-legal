Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy was prepared with the assistance of AI. Care has been taken to ensure its contents accurately reflect FeuFitness's actual data practices as described below.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FeuFitness ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. FeuFitness is a BMI, weight, food, and exercise tracking app.

The short version

  • Most of your data (weight entries, food log, water intake, exercise history, profile details) is stored only on your own device by default. We don't see it, and it isn't sent anywhere unless you turn on Cloud Sync.
  • If you create an account to enable Cloud Sync, that same data is stored securely with our backend provider (Supabase) so it can follow you across devices.
  • Premium features that use AI (Coach chat, AI Meal Scan) send the relevant message or photo to our AI provider (Anthropic) through our own server — never directly from your phone to a third party.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Profile details: height, weight, date of birth/age, sex, ethnicity (used only to select the correct BMI category thresholds and food suggestions), fitness goals, areas to avoid during exercise, unit preference (metric/imperial), and language preference.
  • Weight log entries: the weight values and dates you log.
  • Food log entries: foods you log, including custom entries you type in yourself, and photos you choose to submit for AI Meal Scan.
  • Water intake entries: the amounts and times you log.
  • Exercise and plan activity: which workouts/meditation sessions you mark complete, and any custom notes you add.
  • Messages you send to Coach Lumi (our AI chat feature), if you use it.
  • Account credentials (email and password) if you choose to create an account for Cloud Sync. Passwords are handled by our backend provider's authentication system — we do not see or store your password in plain text.
  • Support messages: anything you submit through the in-app Help/Feedback form, plus an optional contact email if you choose to provide one.

Information collected automatically

  • Step count: read from your phone's own motion sensor (iOS) or a live session counter (Android), used only to display your activity on the Home dashboard. This data is read locally on your device; historical step counts are not uploaded to us.
  • Purchase/subscription status: if you subscribe to Premium, our payment processing partner (RevenueCat) receives transaction information from Apple's App Store needed to verify and manage your subscription (see "Third parties" below).

Information we do not collect

  • We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not access your photo library, contacts, location, or microphone. (Note: on Android, the camera library we use requests microphone permission as a side effect of supporting video capture — FeuFitness never records or transmits audio, and only uses the camera for still photos: barcode scanning and meal photos.)

How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Show you your BMI, weight trend, and related health estimates (BMI category, estimated body fat %, estimated basal metabolic rate) — these are formula-based estimates, not medical measurements, and are clearly labeled as such in the app.
  • Log and display your food, water, and exercise history.
  • Generate calorie targets and a daily meal plan, and suggest exercises based on your preferences.
  • Power the optional AI features (Coach Lumi chat and AI Meal Scan) — see below for how this data is handled.
  • Sync your data across your devices, if you enable Cloud Sync.
  • Process and manage your Premium subscription.
  • Respond to support requests you submit.
  • Show the app in your selected language, including right-to-left layout for Arabic and Urdu.

AI features

If you use Coach Lumi or AI Meal Scan (Premium features), the relevant chat message or meal photo — along with limited context needed to make the response useful, such as your recent weight trend or logging streak — is sent to our backend, which forwards it to our AI provider, Anthropic, for processing. This data is used solely to generate a response and is not used by us to train AI models. We do not send your name, email, or exact identity to the AI provider as part of this process — requests are tied to an anonymous internal user ID.

Data storage and security

Data that stays on your device is protected by your device's own security (including Face ID/passcode lock, if you enable the in-app biometric lock). Data synced via Cloud Sync is stored with Supabase, protected by row-level security so that only your authenticated account can access your own records. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your choices and rights

  • Cloud Sync is optional. The app works fully without ever creating an account.
  • You can delete individual entries (weight, food, water) at any time within the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us through the in-app Help/Feedback form. We will action this within 30 days.
  • You can request a copy of the data we hold about you through the same channel.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA to know what personal information we collect and to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information.

Children's privacy

FeuFitness is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us through the in-app Help/Feedback form so we can delete it.

Health disclaimer

FeuFitness provides general fitness and nutrition estimates for informational purposes only. Nothing in the app constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified health professional regarding your individual health circumstances.

International data transfers

Because our service providers operate internationally, your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for these transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.

Contact us

For any privacy questions, requests, or concerns, please use the Help / Send Feedback option within the app's Settings. This routes your message to us directly without publicly exposing any contact address.
